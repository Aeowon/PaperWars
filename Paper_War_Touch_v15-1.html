How to play ⌄
  1. Take turns drawing isolated islands in the center. They cannot touch an HQ or another island.
  2. Each player begins with a square tank and circular helicopter in their safe HQ.
  3. On your turn, choose Move or Shoot, then touch the center of one of your units.
  4. Quickly swipe a curved line without lifting. The first lift ends your move or shot.
  5. A tank must finish on an island. A helicopter must finish in open space. A failed landing or movement off the page destroys that unit.
  6. Shots must touch an enemy unit's outline. One hit destroys it. No firing from HQ.
  7. First player to eliminate all opposing units wins.
Controls and design notes

The first stroke starts at a unit's center. A slow or paused stroke is cancelled; the turn does not advance. Quick swipes are required; slow strokes are cancelled without taking a turn. For pass-and-play, hand the device to the next player after each action. HQs remain safe, and units cannot re-enter after deploying. Tanks and helicopters cannot shoot from inside their HQ. Shots that miss simply end the turn.

The original island crossing rule for helicopters, shot blocking by islands, friendly fire, and multi-target shots were uncertain. This game uses configurable interpretations; friendly units can be hit by friendly shots.
